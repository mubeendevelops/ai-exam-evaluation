# common-passwords.txt.gz

The 100,000 most common passwords published by the UK National Cyber Security Centre (taken
from Have I Been Pwned breach data), as distributed in SecLists
(`Passwords/Common-Credentials/100k-most-used-passwords-NCSC.txt`, MIT License,
Copyright (c) 2018 Daniel Miessler). Lower-cased, de-duplicated and sorted; downloaded
2026-10-03. Rebuild with `scripts/build_password_list.sh`.
