# english-words.txt.gz

English word list of the OCR selector's lexicon term (P10), built by
`scripts/build_word_list.sh` from SCOWL 2020.12.07 (http://wordlist.aspell.net/): sizes 10–50 of
the english, american, british and proper-name lists, lower-cased, words of two or more letters.
