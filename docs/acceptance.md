# Acceptance — Tarn AI Evaluation (P22)

Date: 7 Oct 2026. Two parts: (1) the running app compared with the prototype `docs/project_idea.html`, screen by screen; (2) the requirement checklist R1–R7 and C8–C30 of requirements v0.7. Measured error rates are in `docs/benchmarks/summary.md`.

How the comparison was made: the prototype served from `docs/` and the app (`make up`, `make seed`, signed in as a seeded DEMO_COM teacher) side by side in Chrome, same window width; every screen of the prototype was opened in both, then the DOM and layout of the app screens were checked at laptop and phone width.

## 1. Prototype comparison

The prototype is the look and the layout; where the build plan or a requirement forced a different shape, the difference is listed with its reason. **D51** (user, 2026-10-03) is the standing rule: the build plan's tab names and scope win, and prototype-only features are added when the user asks.

### Visual gaps found and fixed in P22

| # | Where | Gap | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| V1 | Step 2, AI Segmentation (`PagePanel.tsx`) | With a long booklet (many answers) the right column is thousands of pixels tall. The prototype's page panel is short and stays beside the segments. | The panel is `self-start` and sticky (`lg:sticky lg:top-20`): the page stays in view while the segment list scrolls. |

Everything else compared (header, tab bar, status pill, avatar, banners, glass panels, colours, fonts, filters, detail view, both modals, the three steps, the schema designer, the footer) matches the prototype's look; the differences below are structural.

## 2. Requirement checklist

Status: **Met** (built and tested), **Partly met** (built, with a stated limit), **Open** (not built, or waiting for something the user must supply). Evidence names a test (`path::test`) or a screen. All backend tests are `backend/<package>/tests/...`.

### What remains before a real college can use it

1. Teacher validation of the keys and rubrics (C8) and teacher-marked booklets: every threshold, weight and calibration is a placeholder until then (O29, O54, O60–O63).
2. A university answer booklet to test layout (C18).
3. A Google Cloud account to run `deploy/gcp/` (O102) and a decision on production compute (O100).
4. A UI to save and list blueprints (O20), "Copy to my college" is there but the Schema Designer cannot yet store what it designs.
5. Batch scale (C30): direct-to-bucket uploads (O39, O98) and more than one booklet at a time.
